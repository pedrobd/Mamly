Frequently asked questions

Is Mamly free?

Yes, it’s free, with no subscriptions.

Does it work on iPhone?

For now the app is Android only. On iPhone you can use the web version, but reminders only ring while the page is open.

What happens to my data?

Your reminders are stored only on your phone. There are no Mamly accounts or servers. When you speak into the microphone, the audio is turned into text by Android's speech service (usually Google's). Read the privacy policy.

Does it really ring with the phone locked?

Yes. Mamly uses Android's system alarms. On some brands (Samsung, Xiaomi) you should allow the app to run in the background — the app shows you how.

The voice speaks another language. What do I do?

Install an English voice in your Android settings, under “Text-to-speech output”, and choose Google as the engine. The app helps you get there.

Do I need internet?

Reminders work offline. To understand what you say into the microphone, and for the “natural” voices, Android may need a connection.
