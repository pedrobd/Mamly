Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

In short:

  • Your reminders are stored only on your phone.
  • Mamly has no accounts, no servers of its own and no ads.
  • We don't collect app usage statistics or location data. The website doesn't use cookies.
  • When you use the microphone, the audio is turned into text by Android's speech service (usually Google's).

1. Who we are

Mamly is a spoken-reminders app developed independently in Portugal. For any privacy question, email us at hello@mamly.app.

2. What data exists and where it is kept

  • Reminders (the text, times, repetition and whether they're done) and settings (the name you choose for the voice to call you, the voice, mom mode, language, etc.): stored only in the app's local storage, on your phone. They are not sent to us or to third parties.
  • Diagnostics log: a short list of technical events (for example “alarm set for 16:00”) kept on the phone to help you understand if a reminder failed. It only leaves the phone if you decide to share it (for example, by sending a screenshot).

If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted. Android may include it in your Google account's automatic backup, depending on your phone's settings.

3. Microphone and speech recognition

When you tap the microphone, the app uses the speech recognition service installed on Android (usually Google's) to turn what you say into text. That processing is done by that service, under its provider's privacy policy (for Google: policies.google.com/privacy). Mamly does not record or store audio. The microphone is only used when you activate it or, if you have that option on, for a few seconds after a reminder, to listen for replies such as “done” or “snooze”.

4. The voice that reads reminders

Reminders are read by Android's text-to-speech engine. Some “natural” voices use the internet; in that case, the reminder text is sent to that engine's provider to be turned into speech.

5. Sharing reminders

When you share a reminder, Mamly creates a link that contains the reminder inside the link itself (after the “#” symbol). That part of the address is not sent to any server when the link is opened. The link is sent through the app you choose (for example, WhatsApp), under that app's rules.

6. Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone: to understand reminders you dictate.
  • Notifications: to show reminders with the “Done” and “Snooze” buttons.
  • Alarms & reminders: so reminders ring on time, even with the screen off.
  • Run at startup: to set reminders again after you restart your phone.
  • Internet: used only by Android's speech services and to open links.

7. Website statistics

The mamly.app website (not the app) uses GoatCounter to count visits and button clicks, in aggregate. It uses no cookies, doesn't store your IP address and doesn't follow you across other sites. The content of shared reminders (the part of the link after “#”) is never sent.

8. Cookies and browser storage

The mamly.app website doesn't use cookies. It only stores, in your own browser:

  • the language you chose (Portuguese or English), if you tap the PT/EN button;
  • if you use the web version of the app, your reminders and settings, so it can work.

This is strictly necessary for what you asked for and isn't used to track you or for advertising, so it doesn't need consent. The website statistics (section 7) store nothing in your browser. You can delete this data at any time in your browser settings.

9. Children

Mamly is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Your rights

Because your data stays on your phone, you're in full control: you can edit or delete it in the app, or delete everything by uninstalling it. We don't have a copy of your reminders, so we can't view, correct or recover them.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page, with the update date.
